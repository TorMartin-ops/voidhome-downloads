# Voidhome 3.3.2

A hotfix for 3.3.1.

- The Stone Familiar mines ore again. Before, when its generator made an ore (or tuff, dripstone and other copied stone), the familiar stopped at it until you mined it yourself. Now it mines whatever the generator makes into its chest
- It still never mines a block a player placed
- Its pickaxe still has to be good enough for the ore: iron or better for gold, redstone, diamond and emerald, diamond for ancient debris. With a weaker pickaxe it waits at the ore

Worlds, settings and installs from 3.3.x keep working. If you put the jar in your mods folder by hand, remove the old `voidhome-3.3.1.jar`. Two jars of the same mod stop the game from starting.
