# Voidhome 4.0.0

Voidhome 4.0 "Places": progress you can see on the island and in the void, people who live there, lore you find in blocks, and a late game that changes the world.

**[Watch the 4.0 trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ck0TTUJGIqo)**

## New
- **The Drowned Hall.** A ruined hall near your island. Restore its rooms with bundles of crops, fish, honey, trophies, flowers, sherds and records at each room's Hall Ledger. A finished room is mended overnight and changes the world: a lit dock, a forge corner, guest rooms, a waymark, an Islet Core, the Codex wall
- **Visitors who stay.** They move into homes you build. Favourite biomes, gifts they love and hate, scenes, charters, and each visitor's own look and sounds
- **The world.** Letters to find (some are read aloud), islet scenes and finds to brush, hunt contracts, folklore elites and Legends, ghast ferries and waymarks, and night fishing with depth bands and a dread gauge
- **Progression.** Second routes for the main challenges, forked Oaths, mastery past level 10, the Long Arm perk, raids on demand, the Red Moon's threat budget, a recoverable keepsake grave, the Void Turn after the dragon, and run Ambitions
- **The Ledger.** Voidhome's own screens for every page, reward and parcel, with no chat links. Keyboard support, high contrast and screen-reader text. Press N to open what is waiting
- **Look and sound.** Every block the mod adds has its own texture. Ambience for the void, the Hall, the Post and the Red Moon, and a few calm music tracks in vanilla's rotation (Music slider, and "Voidhome music" in K > Options)
- **Own items.** Every item the mod hands out is its own item with its own texture. No recipe can eat a card or a letter any more, and old ones convert by themselves
- **Visitors who move in live at home.** They sleep in their bed and trade any time with a fresh stall each morning, while the others keep visiting the Post
- **The Red Moon** turns the night sky crimson, with a full red moon and a red cast on moonlit blocks
- **Fixes from playtesting:** lag spikes (including a half-second freeze), visitors drinking invisibility potions, and a multiplayer crash when a connection drops

All of the game's audio (the voices, ambience, sound effects, visitors' sounds and music) was made with ElevenLabs; see NOTICE.md in the zips.

## Install
You need Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Java 25 and Fabric API.

- Windows: extract `voidhome-4.0.0-client.zip` and run `Install.bat`. It updates an older install and keeps your worlds
- Modrinth App or Prism: import `voidhome-4.0.0.mrpack`
- Or put `voidhome-4.0.0.jar` and Fabric API in your mods folder, and remove the old `voidhome-3.3.x.jar`
- Hosting a server: `voidhome-4.0.0-server.zip`, see docs/HOSTING.md

Everyone who plays together needs the same version. Worlds from 3.x load and keep their progress: the first 4.0 start copies the world's Voidhome data to `data/ultimateskyblock/pre-4.0`, and the old config is kept as a `.bak` in `config/`, so you can go back. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
