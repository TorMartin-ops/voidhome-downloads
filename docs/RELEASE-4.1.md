# Voidhome 4.1.0

Voidhome 4.1 "Below": something lives below the island, every skill pulls its weight, ten optional bosses, a Hall to live in, and islets worth the trip.

## New
- **The Eater.** A call from below on your first nights of fishing, a shadow under the void fog, letters from people who heard it. After the dragon it answers: build your own arena deck, cast over the edge at night with the dragon egg in your off hand, and fight it in three phases. Every bite and grab is told two seconds ahead, and it scales for one to three players. If you win, the void gives back what it took
- **Ten optional bosses** from tier II to VI, each with its own drops. Your first kill always teaches you its gift; its weapon or charm comes Plain or Fine, with a chance that rises on every miss. Duplicates fuse at a crafting table, and Pell and Mira want them. None blocks progress; the netherite catalyst already works at half strength before the Wither falls
- **Fishing, perks and the Hunter.** A rod in the start chest and faster bites. Every perk is something you feel (Twin Hook doubles the whole catch, the Buoy casts a second line), capstones hold three charges, and the Hunter's marked monster can no longer hide. A new pact, No Second Dawn: no one wakes
- **A new Hall.** A new building, ruined and restored in every room. Allays gather items for the Hall chest, every room adds a reward (a Lamp, the Copper Hopper and Farm Ledger, a Stock Book, the Waybarrel, a Moon Bell, a Map Table), every bundle gives a gift, and a repair night at the Great Hearth mends each finished room. A 4.0 Hall is re-placed and keeps your blocks
- **Islets.** A first islet in sight of a new world, signal smoke and sound on every islet, an Islet Chart that points at the nearest islet nobody has stood on, and loot that is worth more the farther out you go
- **Light tech.** The Copper Hopper, the Farm Ledger, the Storehouse Lectern, one redstone rule for every Voidhome machine and the Waybarrel
- **Story and sound.** The story is told in the world, with voices, a finale track for the fight and the chorus of the Lost. The echo ambience bed is on by default
- **Also:** hints in order, a "Not yet tried" list in Knowledge, a slightly slower expert pace, and a lag budget for every new system

All of the game's audio (the voices, ambience, sound effects, visitors' sounds and music) was made with ElevenLabs; see NOTICE.md in the zips.

## Install
You need Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5, Java 25 and Fabric API.

- Windows: extract `voidhome-4.1.0-client.zip` and run `Install.bat`. It updates an older install and keeps your worlds
- Modrinth App or Prism: import `voidhome-4.1.0.mrpack`
- Or put `voidhome-4.1.0.jar` and Fabric API in your mods folder, and remove the old `voidhome-4.0.0.jar`
- Hosting a server: `voidhome-4.1.0-server.zip`, see docs/HOSTING.md

Everyone who plays together needs the same version. Worlds from 4.0 load and keep their progress: the first 4.1 start copies the world's Voidhome data to `data/ultimateskyblock/pre-4.1`, and the old config is kept as a `.bak` in `config/`, so you can go back. Your on/off switches are kept when the config is reset to the 4.1 defaults. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
